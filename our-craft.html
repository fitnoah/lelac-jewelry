Buying a diamond should not require a gemology degree. Ours was earned so that yours is not needed. Here is exactly how we choose the stones we sell, and why.

Why we started Le Lac

Le Lac Jewelry was founded by a GIA Graduate Gemologist who kept seeing the same problem: customers were asked to spend serious money on a stone they could not evaluate, guided by sales language instead of evidence. Two diamonds with identical carat weight and color could look completely different on the hand, and most buyers had no way to understand why.

Le Lac exists to close that gap. We sell only lab-grown diamonds, we back them with independent grading from the Gemological Institute of America, and we explain every decision in plain language. If we would not be comfortable showing you the report, we do not sell the stone.

Where a diamond was formed does not determine its beauty. How well it was cut does.

What a lab-grown diamond is

A lab-grown diamond is carbon crystallized into the same cubic structure as a diamond formed deep in the earth. It is not a simulant like cubic zirconia or moissanite. It has the same chemical composition, the same hardness, and the same optical properties as a mined diamond, and gemological laboratories grade it on the same scales.

The difference is the environment. Because lab-grown diamonds are created under controlled conditions rather than mined, they can be offered at a more accessible price. That lets our customers put their budget toward what actually changes how a diamond looks: better cut, better color, or a larger stone.

The GIA report, explained

Every Le Lac diamond over 0.18 carats comes with an independent GIA grading report. GIA does not sell diamonds, which is exactly why its opinion matters. The report documents the four characteristics that define a diamond's quality:

CutHow well the diamond's proportions, symmetry and polish interact with light. For round brilliants, GIA grades cut from Excellent to Poor. This is the factor we weigh most heavily.
ColorHow close the diamond is to colorless, graded on GIA's D to Z scale. Differences between neighboring grades are often invisible without side-by-side comparison under controlled light.
ClarityThe size, number and position of internal and surface characteristics, from Flawless to Included. Many stones in the middle of the scale look clean to the unaided eye.
CaratWeight, not size. A poorly cut diamond can carry hidden weight in its depth and actually look smaller face-up than a lighter, well-cut stone.

What the report does not tell you is just as important: it is not a price appraisal, and it does not describe the setting. It is a scientific record of the stone itself.

Why cut matters most

GIA's cut grading system for round brilliant diamonds is built on years of published research. Its researchers combined computer modeling with more than 70,000 observations of almost 2,300 diamonds by over 300 people, comparing how real viewers judged real stones. The result evaluates seven components together: brightness, fire, scintillation, weight ratio, durability, polish and symmetry.

The three that shape what you see are the first three. Brightness is the white light a diamond returns to your eye. Fire is the flash of spectral color as light disperses. Scintillation is the pattern of sparkle and contrast that moves as the diamond, the light, or you move. A diamond with excellent color and clarity but a mediocre cut will look flat next to a well-cut stone of lower grades. That is why we generally recommend diamonds graded Very Good or Excellent for cut, and why we built the Sparkle Lab on our home page so you can see the difference for yourself.

How we choose every stone

  1. Start with the report. We review the GIA grading report first, with cut grade, polish and symmetry as the first screen.
  2. Look past the grades. Two stones with the same report can still differ. Our gemologist reviews proportions and light performance before a stone is approved.
  3. Balance beauty and value. We steer budget toward the characteristics you can see, and away from upgrades that only show up on paper.
  4. Explain it to you. Every recommendation comes with a plain-language reason. Our assistant can answer questions any time, and our team is available when you want a person.
