The Le Lac Standard

A real diamond, grown with intention.

Lab-grown diamonds share the same crystal structure, chemistry and optical properties as mined diamonds. What sets one diamond apart from another is not where it was formed. It is how well it was cut.

I.

Identical by nature

Chemically, physically and optically the same as a mined diamond. Grown in a controlled environment, which makes exceptional quality more accessible.

II.

Verified by GIA

Every Le Lac diamond over 0.18 carats comes with an independent GIA report covering cut, color, clarity and carat weight.

III.

Cut comes first

Cut drives brightness, fire and scintillation. We favor diamonds graded Very Good or Excellent for the best balance of beauty and value.
